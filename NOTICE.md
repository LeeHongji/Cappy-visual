# Attribution

Cappy-visual adapts the article-illustration workflow, visual DNA, composition categories and prompting/QA approach from **Ian Xiaohei Illustrations**, created by Ian. The upstream MIT license and copyright notice are preserved in `LICENSE`.

The recurring character is **Cappy**, the project owner's supplied white keycap IP. `assets/reference/cappy-turnarounds.png` and `assets/reference/cappy-interactions.png` preserve the supplied originals and are published at the owner's direction. The Cappy artwork is independent of upstream character imagery.

Upstream examples are not distributed with this public repository. The Cappy examples were newly generated for this project. They calibrate the skill's visual style; the illustration workflow invents fresh compositions for new articles.
